# Code of conduct

ROCO follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, criticise work and not people, and leave room for people who are new
to robots or to open source. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted anywhere in the project: issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other place you speak for it.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainers at **CONTACT_EMAIL**. Reports are read by the maintainers only
and kept private. The maintainers decide on the response as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a
correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban.
