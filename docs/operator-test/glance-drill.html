ROCO glance drill

Solo version of task 1. You see a saved picture of the Run screen, then answer from memory. Scene order is shuffled so you do not know which comes next. Your first pass is the one that counts. Repeating teaches you the pictures. These are still images: the drill tests reading the screen, not using it. Offline, nothing is sent anywhere.

Use a new ID such as ME-day7 for a repeat on another day.

How each picture is shown

Before you start, sit as you would at a test: screen at normal distance. When you press Show, look at the whole screen as you would while watching a robot, and then answer. In timed mode press Space, Enter or click only when you could answer both questions.
